# BizPilot Launcher Redesign (revised)

**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Status:** Active direction (supersedes lookbook approach)  
**Scope:** `index.html`, `assets/css/launcher.css`, `assets/js/launcher.js` only

## Goal

Keep the familiar three-demo chooser, but make it feel more premium: dark stage, elevated cards, quieter motion — sharper and less flashy than the original tilted showroom.

## Approach

**Dark premium stage + three elevated cards** (user-chosen after rejecting the lookbook layout).

## Structure

1. **Header** — BizPilot brand left (dominant), intro copy right (“Choose a business demo”).
2. **Three equal cards** — Legal, Jewellery, Grocery in one row (stack on mobile).
3. **Footer** — Minimal copyright line.

No separate scroll lookbook. No 3D tilt, orbit numbers, or heavy glass badges.

## Visual language

- Near-black stage with soft depth + grain (no purple glow)
- Elevated dark cards with sparse gold / rose / green accents
- Fraunces + Outfit typography
- Hover: slight lift, accent border, image scale, CTA arrow shift
- Load fade/rise only; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Links / images

Unchanged paths to the three demos and existing hero images.
